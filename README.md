# 🌍 Global Retail Sales Analysis & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Retail Sales Analysis & Business Intelligence Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze retail sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of **sales, profit, products, customers, regions, and overall business performance**. It helps users understand sales trends, identify high-performing areas, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall retail sales performance.
* Track total sales and profit.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Analyze regional and geographical sales performance.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Identify profitable and underperforming areas.
* Create interactive dashboards for better business decision-making.
